Our Story

COVEN started in 2026 as a university web project among five close friends. What began as a classroom assignment quickly grew into late-night coding sessions, raw apparel sketches, and an obsession with creating something real.

Somewhere between building our storefront and designing our first pieces, we realized we didn’t want our vision to end on grading day—we wanted to wear our own uniform. Named after the strength of a tight-knit collective, COVEN brings together five distinct perspectives unified by unfiltered street culture, digital craft, and heavyweight essentials built with zero compromise.

We started with code—now we’re taking it to the concrete. Welcome to the circle.
