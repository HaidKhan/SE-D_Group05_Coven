Our Story

Coven began in 2024 as a late-night creative experiment between a small circle of friends who were tired of choosing between disposable fast fashion and unattainable luxury streetwear. Working out of a cramped studio with a single screen-printing press and stacks of heavyweight cotton blanks, we set out to craft garments that feel intentional—built with structured boxy silhouettes, deep wine and sun-washed cream palettes, and details made to outlast seasonal trends. What started as twenty hand-numbered hoodies shared within our own circle quickly grew into a tight-knit community bound by shared taste and independent spirit. Today, every drop from Coven still honours that first night: small-batch streetwear designed with patience, worn with quiet confidence, and made for people who dress for themselves.
